About PlantAdvisor PH

PlantAdvisor uses your real-time location and current weather to recommend the best crops to grow in the Philippines.

It considers temperature, humidity, and the planting season for each crop. The database includes rice, sugarcane, root crops, vegetables, and more — all suited for tropical climates.

Data is fetched from Open-Meteo for weather and OpenStreetMap for location names.

Made with for Filipino gardeners.
